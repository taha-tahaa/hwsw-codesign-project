NOTE: the first run of tb_ray_sphere FAILED (2+3 gave 6.5, 9-4 gave 2.5) and
exposed a real bug in fp32_add: the carry path normalized the leading one to
bit 26 while the leading-zero path normalized it to bit 27, and the mantissa
slice sum_n[26:4] matched neither. Fixed to a single bit-26 convention with
mant_raw = sum_n[25:3]. The randomized testbench was added afterwards because
three hand-picked cases are not enough to trust a floating-point unit.
