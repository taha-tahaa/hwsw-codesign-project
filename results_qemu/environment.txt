how the guest was launched (on naranja7, from /scratch/ece882-031)
  qemu-system-x86_64 -m 4096 -smp 8 -cpu host,pmu=on -accel kvm \
    -display none -serial chardev:ser0 \
    -nic user,model=virtio-net-pci,hostfwd=tcp::18231-:22 \
    -drive file=work.qcow2,format=qcow2

  pmu=on is the part that matters: without it the guest has no vPMU and
  perf reports <not supported> for every hardware event.  With it,
  cycles / instructions / cache / branch counters all work.

  work.qcow2 is a qcow2 OVERLAY over the course image, so
  jammy-server-cloudimg-amd64-disk-kvm.img is never written to.

guest measurement caveats, found by testing
  1. Fewer usable vPMU counters than the host.  Requesting six events in
     one perf stat run makes one of them read 0.  All counter numbers in
     the reports were therefore taken in GROUPS OF TWO.
  2. stalled-cycles-frontend / stalled-cycles-backend are <not supported>,
     so a full CPI stack cannot be built in the guest.
  3. CPython 3.10 predates the -X perf trampoline (3.12+), so perf cannot
     resolve Python function names here - its flame graphs show CPython
     internals.  Python-level attribution comes from cProfile and py-spy.
