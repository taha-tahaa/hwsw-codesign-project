Top Python functions by self time, measured INSIDE THE QEMU GUEST.

Source: py-spy 0.4.2, --rate 999, sampled on the real workload.

Why py-spy and not perf: the guest runs CPython 3.10, which predates the
-X perf trampoline (3.12+), so perf cannot resolve Python function names here -
its flame graphs show CPython internals only (flamegraph_perf_*.svg).
py-spy walks CPython's frame objects itself and needs no interpreter support,
so it gives Python-level attribution in the guest (flamegraph_pyspy_*.svg).

SAMPLING vs INSTRUMENTATION, reproduced in the guest
----------------------------------------------------
py-spy (sampling)  puts move_to_front at 15.69%
cProfile (counting) puts it at 0.223 s of 2.215 s = 10.1%

Instrumentation charges per CALL; sampling charges per CYCLE. move_to_front is
a function that is simply slow inside - three list slices and two concatenations
per call - so a call-count profiler under-reports it. Same disagreement was
seen on the host with perf (19.28% vs ~8%).
